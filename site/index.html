Methodology

  • Every pitch posted on the club's LinkedIn page is tracked automatically.
  • Entry price: the closing price of the first trading session after the post is published (the same day if it comes out before the close).
  • BUY = long; SELL = short (gains when the price falls). The position is held until the end of the stated horizon, then frozen.
  • Returns include dividends and are in each stock's own currency (no FX effect).
  • Each pitch is compared with its home market index (DAX for Germany, STI for Singapore, S&P 500 for the US…).
  • Price data: Yahoo Finance.
