Methodology

  • Every pitch posted on the club's LinkedIn page is added to the tracker, and its prices are then updated automatically several times a day.
  • Entry price: the share price stated in the pitch itself. Each pitch is tracked from the last market close before the post, and its benchmark index from that same close.
  • BUY = long; SELL = short (gains when the price falls). The position is held until the end of the stated horizon, then frozen.
  • Returns include dividends and are in each stock's own currency: a euro stock is compared with the S&P 500 without converting euros to dollars (no FX effect).
  • Benchmark: the S&P 500 Total Return index (dividends reinvested, like the stocks), for every pitch. Each pitch's detail also shows how it did against its home market index (DAX for Germany, STI for Singapore…); except for the DAX, those home indices leave dividends out.
  • Club portfolio: equal weight in every open pitch, rebalanced daily, with daily returns compounded (time-weighted). Adding a new pitch therefore doesn't move the curve. A pitch leaves the portfolio when its horizon ends.
  • Prices are refreshed during the day, so the latest price can be an intraday one (marked “intraday”) until the market closes. A ⚠ marks a stock with no new price for a trading day or more (market holiday or data delay).
  • Annualised returns are only shown once a pitch has been held for 3 months: over a few days they are meaningless.
  • Price data: Yahoo Finance.
